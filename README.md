 Company Website

## Overview

The Construction Site Management & Company Website is a full-stack web application developed for a construction and building materials production company.

The main purpose of this system is to provide a professional online presence for the company while creating a digital platform for managing construction-related information, company services, projects, and business activities.

The system is designed to support construction companies involved in:
- Building construction
- Road construction
- Aggregate production
- Building materials supply
- Engineering services

The platform allows customers, partners, and stakeholders to understand the company's capabilities, ongoing projects, services, and contact information.

## Project Objectives

The main goals of this project are:

- To develop a professional company website for a construction organization.
- To showcase company services, completed projects, and ongoing activities.
- To improve communication between the company and potential customers.
- To provide a digital platform for promoting construction and building material production services.
- To create a scalable foundation for future business management modules.


 Key Features

 Company Profile Management
- Company introduction and background.
- Mission, vision, and core values.
- Management and organizational information.

 Services Showcase
- Construction services.
- Road infrastructure development.
- Building material production.
- Engineering solutions.

 Project Portfolio
- Display completed construction projects.
- Project descriptions.
- Project images and details.

 Contact Management
- Company contact information.
- Customer inquiry section.
- Location and communication channels.

 Responsive Design
- Mobile-friendly interface.
- Optimized user experience across different devices.
